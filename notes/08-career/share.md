# Share what you learn

*Portfolio & Career* · [Open in the interactive roadmap](https://abbasisarah.github.io/roadmap/#share)

> **In one sentence:** Sharing what you learn, through posts, reels and talks, builds your name, and teaching is the fastest way to understand something yourself.

## Key ideas
- Explain one idea at a time, with one picture.
- Show your process, including mistakes; people relate to it.
- Link every post to a project or the code.
- Consistency beats perfection: one post a week compounds.

## Example
```markdown
Post template
1. Hook: "I trained a neural network without TensorFlow. Here's what I learned."
2. One idea, explained simply, with a visual.
3. Result in a number.
4. Link: GitHub / live demo.
5. Question to the audience.
```

## Common mistakes
- Waiting until you're an "expert" to start.
- Posts with no link to your work.
